Kirtan Player

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Kirtan Player account at any time, from the app or by email. Listening never needed one.

From the app

  1. Open Kirtan Player and sign in
  2. Go to the Saved tab
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm

It happens straight away.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and password
  • Your saved shabads
  • Your playlists

What is kept

If you tagged recordings, those tags stay in the archive, because other people listen to them. Your name is removed from them. If you would like something you tagged removed as well, email us.

By email

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email kirtanplayer@beejaysoft.com from the address on your account with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
