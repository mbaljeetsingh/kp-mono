Kirtan Player

Privacy Policy

Kirtan Player on the web, iOS and Android. Last updated 6 October 2026.

The short version

Listening needs no account and collects nothing about you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. An account exists only so your saved shabads and playlists follow you between devices.

What we store

  • Without an account: your saved shabads, theme and language stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • With an account: your email address, your password (stored hashed, never readable), your saved shabads and your playlists.
  • If you contribute by tagging recordings: what you tag is published for everyone to listen to, and recorded as yours.

Account data is held by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. We use it only to run Kirtan Player. We do not sell it, share it or use it for advertising.

Services the app talks to

  • sgpc.net — the audio streams straight from the Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee's archive. Like any website, it sees your IP address when your device fetches a recording.
  • BaniDB — Gurbani text and translations.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email. See Delete your account.

Children

Kirtan Player is suitable for everyone, and listening asks nothing of anyone. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. Anything that collects more than it does today would be said here first.

Terms of Use

Kirtan Player is free, and provided as is. The recordings belong to the SGPC and are streamed from its public archive; we host no audio. Tagging work contributed to the archive may be shown to everyone and kept after your account is gone, without your name. Please do not misuse the service or attempt to disrupt it.

Contact
